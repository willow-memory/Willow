# hook-feedback's words: what marks a prompt as a correction to the assistant.
# Copy it beside your record and edit it; point --words at your copy.
#
#   + PHRASE   a correction when the prompt starts with it
#   ~ PHRASE   a correction wherever it is in the prompt
#   - PHRASE   never a correction when the prompt starts with it (read first)
#
# A phrase ends at a word's end: "no" is not "now" or "nobody". Case and
# curly apostrophes don't matter.
+ no
+ wrong
+ nope
+ not what i asked
+ that's not what i
~ i said
- no thanks
- no thank you
- no worries
- no problem
- no rush
- no hurry
- no need
