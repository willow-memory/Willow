# A first-day words list for kernel.py, in its own format: one of your words,
# then the words your files might use for it, a comma between. Copy it, keep
# what fits your files, cut the rest, add your own:
#
#   python adapters/stdlib/starter-words/starter.py copy --to ~/my-words.txt
#   python kernel.py init --box my-record --pile FOLDER --key my.key --words ~/my-words.txt
