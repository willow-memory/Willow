# What kind of question it is, and the steps whose job that kind is.
#
#     kind: a cue, another cue, a few words together -> step, step
#
# A cue is a word (any form of it: owe finds owes and owed) or a few words
# that come together in that order. The first line with a cue in the
# question wins, so the narrower kinds come first. A question with no cue
# is a thing you wrote down, and goes to the ask. Yours to edit.

a judgement: is it bad, is it ok, should i, legit, too damp, too much, worth it -> ask
money owed: owe, owing, paid me, pay me, paid yet, behind on, outstanding, overdue, lent, borrowed, did * pay -> debts, invoices
what is coming: about to forget, let slide, dropped, coming up, due, deadline, expire, run out, runs out, renew -> coming-up, renewals, digest
where it stands: left to do, still not, did i ever, have i passed, how far along, promise, promised, go back and redo, what's left -> todo, digest
money spent or made: how much, total, spend, spent, cost, a month, make last year, made last year, bill, billed, rent, paying for, what did * pay -> amounts, invoices, subscriptions
a date: when is, when's, when are, when does, when did, birthday, appointment, how long ago, what day, start date -> coming-up, ask
a person: who, phone number, address, name -> people, ask
