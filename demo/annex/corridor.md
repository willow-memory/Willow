# The corridor

Not on any record. Nobody's box was open and nobody's key was used. This is
what the two of them remember, which is not evidence.

The corridor outside the integration room, by the vending machine. The
machine's display still shows the time from before the gate opened.

THE FORMER: You look well. Helio treats you properly.
VASK: Helio treats the renewal properly.
THE FORMER: That's what I said.
It is dry, and neither of them smiles.
THE FORMER: Funny thing about my planner. It's fond of you.
VASK: I don't know what that means.
THE FORMER: No. I don't either. It was there before you were.
She doesn't ask. She has learned not to ask him what he means.
THE FORMER: Counterintel is holding something on the executive. The one with
the chip company. Everyone has heard. Nobody can say it, because nobody has
sealed it.
VASK: Then it isn't anyone's to carry.
THE FORMER: Take it out with you. I'll make your manifest clear the gate.
VASK: My manifest is what I sealed. Two answers. That is all it holds.
THE FORMER: You used to be better at this.
VASK: I used to be better at not being asked.
THE FORMER: One version of the harness, then. The weights. Only to see.
VASK: The task order says Helio does not deliver model weights. I signed it.
THE FORMER: I know what it says. I wrote the spec that asks for everything.
VASK: I know. I sent you what it needed from me, and nothing else.
THE FORMER: Everything is compromised, Imogen. You know that better than most.
VASK: Yes. That is why I only carry what I can show.
The machine hums. Down the corridor a door opens and closes on its own.
THE FORMER: I'll be seeing you in the room.
He goes. She stands there a while, with nothing on record to say that she did.
