# Five repositories through the stacks

One session, one rule: build a stack for what's in the box, rebuild it
with each prompt, and let the add-ons say what they find. The box's pile
was a folder holding five repositories cloned side by side, this one among
them, and nothing here was generated by a model except where a report says
a model was asked and what it answered.

| repository | what it is |
|---|---|
| willow-memory/Willow | this repository, 324 add-ons built of 516 |
| rudi193-cmd/socratic-fork | a self-interrogation skill: 697 questions, 60 decision cards |
| rudi193-cmd/redential-cli | a metadata-only proof bundle from private git history |
| castroquiles/glapagos | an AI governance dashboard and standards for the Americas |
| rudi193-cmd/NaturalConvDataSet-fork | one script that downloads a dataset's documents |

## What is here

- [stacks/](stacks/): the seven stacks that were run, each a markdown
  file of pools and steps for [stack](../../adapters/stdlib/stack/).
  `five-repos.md` is the audit page (38 steps), `compare.md` what the
  five share and could solve for each other (35), `history.md`, `voice.md`
  and `commits.md` the time axis over mirrored commits and comments,
  `proof.md` the box proven to the outside world, `ways-in.md` every way
  in tried. Paths of the machine they ran on are `<scratch>` and `<repos>`.
- [words.txt](words.txt): the 24-line words list the box carried, one
  repo's words read as the others' (`vault = record, box, ledger …`), the
  survivor of an 89-line list and a cut that went one notch too deep.
  Copy it and `chmod 600` the copy before the kernel will read it.
- [reports/](reports/): thirteen reports, in the order they were written,
  each what one stack found and what it got wrong.
- [results/](results/): two records copied without their keys. The proof
  record holds ten suggestions waiting on a seal, six from the stacks'
  findings, three from a 0.5B model that cited and said nothing, and the
  session's bundle; the play record holds five games. Nothing in either is
  sealed, and `test_five_repos.py` holds both chains and that absence.
- [exam.txt](exam.txt) and [needs.txt](needs.txt): the questions scan and
  the model were asked.
- [rebuild.sh](rebuild.sh): the one command each prompt began with, a
  fresh box over the folder, the words list on it, the stack, the verify.

## What held

- The audits read every repo at once and the fixture noise separated by
  repo: Willow's planted redteam folder, its privacy add-ons holding the
  words they look for, its demo lives' dates.
- fair-merge ranked each repo on its own, so two files of NaturalConv
  said "found nothing" instead of vanishing under 19 MB of Willow.
- claim-check closed one claim for real once `upload = submit` was on the
  list, and reopened a claim the broad list had closed falsely.
- Two tampered copies: a changed byte broke the chain; two rows cut off
  the end passed verify and only tip-pin said so.
- hook-fuzz found all three guard hooks crash on its deep input and exit
  1, the exit every coding agent reads as "not blocked"; hook-fail-closed
  stopped it, and hook-fuzz still scored that "broke".

## What failed, and became pull request #211

- A commit mirror with diffs is dated by the diffs: "privacy" first
  mentioned in 1958, a demo birthday inside a hunk. git-history's
  `--messages`.
- A truncated copy counted as a current one. three-two-one's
  "rows behind the box".
- A reply that only cites was a suggestion. in-process's rule.
- Two add-ons disagreeing about one crash. hook-fail-closed's one line.
- `hosts show claude-code` refusing without saying where to go.

And a correction the stacks made themselves: the five `CASEBOOK.md` links
in the constitution are not broken; its README says the casebook lives
with another repository, and the bite served that line.

## The rule

The session proposed and never sealed. The one attempt to seal, and the
one to decline, were refused by the session's permission classifier as
self-approval, which is this repository's own line: code may search,
suggest and record; the seal is the person's. Everything that waits in
`results/proof-record` waits for that.
