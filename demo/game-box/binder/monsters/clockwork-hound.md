# Clockwork hound

Cogwen's guard dog, wound once a night. Made up for this binder.

Grit 3, Wit 0, Heart 0, Weird 1.
Hits: 6. Armour: brass, so a blade does half.
Bite: 2d6 + Grit; on a strong hit it holds on.
Weakness: water in the gears. A bucket stops it for a round.
Winding down: after six rounds it sits, ticking, until someone winds it.
