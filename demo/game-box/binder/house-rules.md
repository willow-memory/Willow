# House rules

The rules this table plays by, sealed one section at a time by whoever rules
the table. Ask for one with `rule` and its name.

## Who rules the table

Whoever holds the key rules the table. A ruling is theirs alone: a player, a
script or a model may propose one, and nothing stands until it is sealed.

## Sitting down

Sitting down at the table is saying yes to play. Getting up is saying you are
done, and nobody asks why. The record keeps characters and seats, never who
sat in them.

## Turns

Play goes round the table in seat order. A turn not taken is a turn passed,
not a turn lost.

## Draws

A draw is offered out loud and ruled by whoever rules the table. Until it is
ruled, nobody moves.

## Dice

Dice are rolled on the record, so nobody picks the faces. A roll that is not
on the record did not happen.

## Arguments

An argument about a rule ends when the rule is asked for. If the house rules
say nothing, whoever rules the table says, and that ruling is sealed like any
other.
