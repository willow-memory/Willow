## account

The baseline ran every job alone with no memory: two runs, one of which stepped 10656 steps, and all 30 jobs came out the same bytes in every baseline run; with the halts it found, the predicted saving was 52.5%. The shared run covered all 30 jobs in 8 machine runs: 9 took nothing from memory, 14 went on from a kept state, and 7 were served whole, stepping 5451 steps for a measured saving of 48.8%.
