## the reports

### the sweep
220 jobs over 22 trajectories, dealt to 5 machines in 2 rounds; a state kept every 100 steps.

### the baseline: every job alone, no memory
2 runs. One run stepped 1256518 steps.
220 of 220 jobs came out the same bytes in every baseline run.
With the halts the baseline found, the predicted saving is 73.7%.

### the shared run
220 of 220 jobs were run, by 10 machine runs.
22 took nothing from memory, 169 went on from a kept state, 29 were served whole.
It stepped 336379 steps. Measured saving: 73.2%.

### the check
1305 kept states were stepped again; 1305 came out the same bytes.
20 served jobs were run afresh; 20 came out the same bytes.

### the project's gravity as it is written
5 machines ran it. 14 of 220 jobs came out the same bytes on every one.

### the machines
Kinds of processor that ran the jobs: 3; amd x86_64 avx2; intel x86_64 avx2; intel x86_64 avx512.
