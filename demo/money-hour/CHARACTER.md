# The money hour: four people at one table

Fiction, every person in it. Each comes from another of willow's demos and
keeps the life given there; here they meet once a month on a video call
about money that Teo hosts. Nothing they say is advice, and the call's first
rule is Teo's: if you repeat a number, say where it came from.

| Who | From | Here | Their folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Teo Marinescu** | [teodora](../teodora/) | Leeds, self-employed bookkeeper. Hosts the call, keeps the minutes, and checks what people bring against the source. | minutes of three calls, notes for November, a client file, her to-do list, her own wager |
| **Bernie Halloran** | [bernadette](../bernadette/) | Ballarat, retired ICU nurse. Both pensions sit in AusPrime Super, so she follows US jobs figures she never used to. Gerry brings home numbers from the Men's Shed. | super notes, the roof, Aoife's 21st, her wager |
| **Hollis Varga** | [hollis](../hollis/) | Larkfield, piano tuner. Hears things on the van radio and reads a newsletter; his accountant Dana does his books. | October and November notes, the newsletter's summary beside the paragraph it summarised, his week, his father, his wager |
| **Marisol Archuleta** | [ofrenda](../ofrenda/) | Española, keeps her grandmother Femia's ofrenda. Femia's savings bonds matured this year; her cousin Lupe in Alamosa has opinions. | her grandmother's bonds, what Lupe says, the ofrenda, her wager |

Today is 2026-11-05. Their own plans may run past it (a VAT return, a
roofer, a van service); nothing they say the world did is dated after it.

## How their lives write themselves down

Short Markdown notes, a folder each, first line always saying they are
fiction. Every line that names a real figure, rate, official or company is a
frozen line from [questions.json](questions.json), word for word, and
`make_life.py --check` refuses any other. The one file in them that is not
theirs is Hollis's copy of a 10-K paragraph, read from the markets mirror
word for word and labelled as a copy.
