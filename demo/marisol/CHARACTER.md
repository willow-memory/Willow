# Marisol Ortiz Beltrán

Fiction, made for a demo on 2026-11-12; no real person is behind her. The
school, the companies and everyone around her are invented too. Phones are
`787-555-01xx` and mail is at `example.org` and `example.com`.

## Who she is

- **41**, computer-science and robotics teacher at a public high school in
  Ponce, called here Escuela Superior La Ceiba.
- Keeps one folder of everything: lesson plans, an outage log kept since
  Hurricane Maria, receipts, emails, her father's interview, and a half-finished
  grant draft to Puente Digital (a fictional nonprofit that donates devices).
- Good at spotting a weak source in a minute, and at teaching students to do
  the same. Wants to teach the AI-literacy course without asking permission
  every year.

## Her cast

| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| Ernesto Beltrán, 74 | Her father; retired from a pharmaceutical plant (fictional: Laboratorios Caribe del Sur) that closed in 2004. Repairs old radios. |
| Camila Rosario, 16 | Student, captain of the robotics club. |
| Héctor Delgado | The school's principal. |
| Inés Quiñones | Program officer at Puente Digital; asks her to source every claim. |
| Tomás Beltrán | Her brother in Orlando; sent $6,000 for the battery bank. |

Voice rules (from the character base): hardship is dates, logs and logistics,
never suffering shown for effect; the expertise is hers and nobody rescues her;
Spanish is spelled with its accents and kept whole, never decoration inside
English; and a Puerto Rican reader reads it before it goes beyond its author.
