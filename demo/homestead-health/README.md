# A box for health: the homestead-affairs repositories as one pile

Started 2026-11-11, on the maintainer's word, to have something to ask
while talking through what a pile of willows would look like in health
care. A start, not a study: nothing is frozen and nothing here is a
result.

**The pile** is the six repositories of `github.com/homestead-affairs`,
each at one commit, named in [sources.json](sources.json): the household
engine, its health, ledger and law modules, the organisation's own
pages, and its list of software a person owns. 397 files. None of their
text is kept here; [rebuild.sh](rebuild.sh) fetches them again at those
commits, builds a fresh box on a scratch key outside this repository,
and asks it [questions.txt](questions.txt) and then
[guardian-questions.txt](guardian-questions.txt).

    demo/homestead-health/rebuild.sh SCRATCH

**Why these.** The health module already holds what the talk had only
reached by argument: an emergency card that is authored and never
computed, a subject known by an opaque id, a name at a higher rung when
the subject is a minor, a log that carries the act and not the content.
The box lets a question be put to that design and answered from its own
lines, with the file and line each time.

## The first asks

Twelve questions, kept as they came in [results/asks.txt](results/asks.txt):
the question, what the kernel said, and where the first passage it
served is. Eleven were served a passage; one found nothing.

What that does and doesn't say:

- **Served is not answered.** The kernel serves a passage that holds half
  the question's words. "What is on the emergency card" came to the
  plan's own paragraph on it. "Who holds the key when the person cannot"
  came to a passage about a pinned reference snapshot, which is not an
  answer: the words were there and the matter was not.
- **"Break glass access in an emergency" found nothing**, and that is
  the pile's answer. These repositories don't use the phrase. What they
  have in its place is the card: something the household chose to let
  leave, exported like any record, with the act logged and the content
  not.
- **Guardianship is in the pile, as a question left open.** The health
  plan says that for a minor the operator is guardian and holds the
  file, and names another project's `guardian_of` edges as prior art
  for the day it is taken up. No question here was worded to find it; a
  search for the word did.

## What came next

- [GUARDIANSHIP.md](GUARDIANSHIP.md): holding a record for someone

- [COMPARED.md](COMPARED.md): homestead's rungs and gates beside
  willow's seal and record, and what a willow that held health records

## What it is not

Not a person's records: there is no patient in this pile, only the
design of a thing that would hold one. Not a judgment of the homestead
repositories, which are read and not changed. And not the kernel's rank
loosened to find more: a question that finds nothing is left finding
nothing.
