# Code of conduct

Willow is built in the open, by whoever turns up. This is how we treat each
other while we do it.

## What we do

- Assume the other person means well, and ask before deciding they don't.
- Say what's wrong with the work, never with the person who made it.
- Credit what others did, and say plainly what you didn't check.
- Welcome questions, including ones that have been asked before.
- Accept a "no" on a change with grace, and say why when you give one.

## What we don't

- Harassment, insults, or attacks on anyone, for any reason, in any place
  the project lives: issues, pull requests, reviews, comments and commits.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Sharing someone's private information, including anything from their own
  files, without their clear consent.
- Pushing on after someone has asked you to stop.

## If something happens

Tell the maintainer privately: through GitHub, by a private report from the
repository's **Security** tab, or a direct message to rudi193-cmd. You'll be
taken seriously and kept private.

The maintainer may edit or remove anything that breaks this, and may ask
someone to step away from the project for a while or for good, saying why.
This applies to the maintainer too.
