**VIII.4 — Always a draft.** This constitution is never closed. Every amendment that enters force makes the next draft, and no draft is final: what this law governs will change, and a law that declares itself finished is routed around. An amendment enters force when the deterministic checks hold and the maintainers accept it. The maintainers are the quorum of VIII.1; while there is one, that one's acceptance is both the quorum and the Operator Key. An amendment must leave the law better enforced than it found it: every clause it adds or changes is cited by an enforcement artifact and attacked by a test, or it may not enter force. Nothing leaves the record: an amendment that replaces another names it, and both are kept. Article 0 stays beyond reach (VIII.3).
