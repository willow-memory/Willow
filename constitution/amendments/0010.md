**VI.8 — The record keeps acts, not words.** A question put to the person's files, and the passages it was served, are kept in the record by their hash and their place, never by their text. The record says that a question was asked, when, what it was served and what was done about it; the words stay in the person's files and the person's head. What the person enters on purpose to be recorded, such as a decision and its reason, is theirs to keep, and is kept as they wrote it. Raw, in VI.6, is every event unsmoothed, not every word kept: the hash is the event's raw form.
