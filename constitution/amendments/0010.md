**VI.8 — The record keeps acts, not words.** What a person asked, and what they were answered, is kept in the record by its hash and its place, never by its text. The record says that a question was asked, when, what it was served and what was done about it; the words stay in the person's files and the person's head. Raw, in VI.6, is every event unsmoothed, not every word kept: the hash is the event's raw form.
