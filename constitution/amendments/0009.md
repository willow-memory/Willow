**V.10 — A no is final.** A person's refusal is made with their key, as an approval is, and recorded. What they refused is not offered to them again, and is never approved over the refusal; a changed proposal is a new proposal and needs its own act. A refusal that does not prove under the person's key counts for nothing (§0.4), and what no one refused or approved is still the person's (§0.6).
