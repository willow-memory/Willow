**VIII.1 — Propose, check, accept.** Any agent may propose an amendment; the proposal is recorded. The proposal must clear an evidence floor: the deterministic checks, held to the standard of Article IV. It enters force when the maintainers, who are the quorum, accept it, and that acceptance is the Operator Key (VIII.4); while there is one maintainer, a supermajority is that one. An amendment takes effect only upon recording — and only if its required compliance tests (Appendix B) remain valid; an amendment that breaks its own tests may not enter force.
