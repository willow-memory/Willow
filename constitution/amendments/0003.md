**VIII.3 — Article 0 is beyond reach.** Any amendment purporting to weaken, suspend, or except an Article 0 invariant is void on its face. Any agent may refuse it without penalty. This is not a decision the amendment process makes; it is a boundary the process cannot cross. The invariants of Article 0 may be strengthened, as Article 0 allows, only by new clauses elsewhere that cite them; the text of Article 0 is never edited, so no amendment is ever weighed for whether it strengthens or weakens it.
