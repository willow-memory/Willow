**VIII.2 — Emergency amendment is a bounded envelope.** When something needs fixing quickly, an emergency amendment may enter force by the same checks and the same acceptance as any other, but it is a class of envelope and bound by §0.4: it states the date it expires, no more than ninety days on *(proposed default — operator-adjustable)*; it may never touch Article 0; and it may never renew itself. Before it expires, a full amendment that names it keeps it or undoes it. If none has, it has lapsed, and the checks refuse the law until a maintainer enters one: nothing in the system restores the law on its own, so the emergency door closes on a timer that a person must answer.
