**V.11 — Offered once, counted once.** A suggestion is offered to the person once. What already waits on them, what they approved and what they refused is not offered again: it is refused where it would be offered, and the refusal is recorded with its reason. What waits on the person is counted once for each thing they have to decide, and every surface that reports it counts the same way. A changed suggestion is a new suggestion, and needs its own act (V.10).
