**VI.7 — Every change carries its account.** A change to the system is recorded with what it changed, by the act that made it, beside the record and never in place of it. A known defect is recorded as a check that shows it, expected to fail; it leaves the record only when that check passes, and its leaving is recorded as a fix. A defect that is only described is not known, and one that leaves without a recorded fix was not fixed.
