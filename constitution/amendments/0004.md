**IV.9 — A check that cannot fail is no check.** Before a check is counted as enforcing anything, it is shown to fail: the thing it checks is broken on purpose, and the check must catch the break. A break no check catches is recorded with the reason it does not matter, or the check is not yet a check; a recorded reason that no longer holds is itself a failure.
