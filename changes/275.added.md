Two real-data demos. `demo/markets-2026-11-05` fetches a broad equities and macro pile once from public sources (FRED, Treasury, the Fed's FOMC documents, CBOE, Fama-French, IMF, World Bank, ECB, OECD, and SEC EDGAR's filing index, XBRL company facts and latest 10-K for S&P 500 companies) by the snapshot add-on, pins each source's SHA-256 in sources.json, sets error bodies aside, and mirrors the pile for the kernel. `demo/money-hour` puts four people from the other demos at one table and holds every market figure, quote and wager in their files to that record, with three stacks (money-hour, fed, filings). snapshot gains `--contact EMAIL`, sent in the User-Agent to the address alone and never written down, for publishers like SEC EDGAR that refuse a client naming none.
