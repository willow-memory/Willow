`equations`, `subtitles` keep the mirror contract. An equation's passage is
placed on the .tex file's lines from the paragraph before it to the end of
the one after, and its citation line is held to cite a line among them; a
cue on its timing line to its last line of text, each cue placed, so a chunk
the kernel cuts from a long transcript is at its own cue. The contract lets
a map place lines inside a passage, and holds a passage's own citation line
to its place
