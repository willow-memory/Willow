`agent-transcript` and `cursor-chats`: a session too long for the kernel to read (over 900,000 bytes as text; the kernel reads 1,000,000) is written in parts, X.txt, X.2.txt, and so on, as `ai-code-tracking` names its parts, so a pile reads them in order and the longest sessions are no longer left out. Parts are cut between turns (a turn longer than a part is cut at a line end, and a line longer than a part between characters); each starts with the session header and `part N of M`, and the turn numbers go on across parts. A session that fits is written as before. A rerun removes a stale X.N.txt left by a longer run, and nothing else.
