`dice-tower`: a die dropped in a tower. `drop` puts only the SHA-256 of a fresh secret on the record and writes the secret to a die file outside the box; `out` puts the secret on the record and throws the dice from it, in play-dice5e's notation and stream, so the faces are settled at the drop by a secret nobody reading the record has: no roll can be foreseen, steered by a row written first, or shopped for (a die comes out once). `check` holds every die out to its drop and names every die still inside; `page` draws the tower, the tray and whether they hold, as one HTML page with nothing fetched. A commitment and its reveal, a cup, drawn as a dice tower. Found at Die-Namic's table, where a roll seeded by the record's tip could be read off the record before it was thrown, and a game master's yes or no chose the next roll. The die file is kept first, made new and readable only by you in one step, and its hash goes on the record after; a record that won't take the row takes the file away again, so no die is ever on the record that nobody holds. 0.1.1.
