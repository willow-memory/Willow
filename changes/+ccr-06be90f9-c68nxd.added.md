`play-dice`, `game-master`: a roll thrown with real dice, said and kept as said. `play-dice said DICE FACES` (4,6 for 2d6) writes a `roll` row whose generator is `said`, with no seed: one face for each die, each on it, the total theirs, and every way it is shown says "as said"; its honesty is the table watching, as the constitution's VI.8 keeps what a person enters on purpose. `check` holds a said roll to its dice, and `check(rows)` refuses one, so a game that holds rolls to seeds alone (play-backgammon, play-liars-dice) stays as strict; `check(rows, said=True)` takes them. `game-master act STAT --said FACES` reads the beat from the faces said, and its check takes said rolls. And `game-master act STAT --tower DIEFILE` brings the roll out of a 2d6 dropped in `dice-tower` when the moment was posed, so no row written between (an offer, a call answered) chose it and nobody reading the record knew it first; its check holds every die out of the tower to its drop. play-dice 0.1.1, game-master 0.2.3.
