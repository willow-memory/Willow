`cup`: dice shaken into a cup. `shake` puts only the SHA-256 of a fresh secret on the record and writes the secret to a cup file outside the box; `lift` puts the secret on the record and throws the dice from it, in play-dice5e's notation and stream, so the faces are settled at the shake by a secret nobody reading the record has: no roll can be foreseen, steered by a row written first, or shopped for (a cup is lifted once). `check` holds every lift to its cup and names every cup still down. Found at Die-Namic's table, where a roll seeded by the record's tip could be read off the record before it was thrown, and a game master's yes or no chose the next roll. 0.1.0.
