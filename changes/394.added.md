`play-fight5e`: the Aetheris setting's 5e encounters (saves, resistances,
recharging specials, stun and blind) fought blow by blow on dice dropped in
dice-tower, each row naming the dice behind it; `check` fights them again from
the record alone, `board` draws one from its rows, and `sim` sweeps an
encounter on seeded dice without writing anything. Planned as a stdlib
add-on; built in deps, for the dice library
