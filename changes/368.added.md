Draft 0.18.0, VI.9, read where they are kept: a box may read its files from several places where they are (`init --from NAME=FOLDER`, or `NAME=@LIST` for a list of files), with or without its pile folder, never copying them; each passage is named under its source. The same in all five ports, held to the vectors, and a fourteenth beat. Add-ons read a box through `kernel.pile_of_box`, `kernel.folder` (which refuses a box of sources it would read half of) and `kernel.place`.
