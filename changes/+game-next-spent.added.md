`spent`: how much you spent on one thing in a year, from the money-out lines of your statements that say it, added up with each line cited by file and line. A minus or a money-out column is money out; a plus, or an amount with no sign, is never added (the unsigned are listed apart); the same payment in two files counts once; currencies are never added together; "this year", "last year" and "financial year" (July start, or `--year financial:MM`) pick the period, and it is always printed. Through the words list, so "electricity" finds ORIGIN ENERGY.
