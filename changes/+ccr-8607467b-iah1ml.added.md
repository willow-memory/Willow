index-cache keeps the index of a list's files where they are kept (`--from @LIST`), and of everything a box reads, its pile and every source (`--box BOX`), named as the kernel names the box's passages. So a box laid from pointers (`init --from`) is answered from an index too: claude-code's hook checks an index against the box's whole manifest, and its `setup` prints the `--box` line. batch-ask's scan gains `build_from`, the index of passages already read.
