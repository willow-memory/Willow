`log-kit`, the engine the logs share: your notes' dated lines, read by a
short spec of words, kinds, a value and a period, summed up as lines, a total
or an average, so a new log is a spec, not a program. Each log kept its own
copy of `entries`; mood is the first moved onto it, its output the same bytes
on a folder of 66 scored days by month and by week. Run on its own, `--words`
makes a log of any words
