`life-box`: `ask` bites every question through the `readings` add-on (`readings.py bite`) in place of `kernel.py bite`, at the person's today, and prints its two hints under the question as `see readings: WHY  WHERE`, kept in the table's `readings` folder, a file a question, and shown again by `recap`, the way `spent`'s output is kept. Readings runs the kernel's own bite, so the passages served, the bite row written and the exit code are the kernel's (a test compares the two over the same box, byte for byte): a proposal still cites the last bite, and a hint is never proposed or sealed. Where readings gives no answer the kernel bites the question as before, nothing is hinted, and recap says so. On Odile's box "something that tells me who's due" gets the seven in her tuning log (*gone by, nothing written since*) and "Dad's weight" Laszlo's 2.5 kg week (and the care log's pulse beside it, the one stray). No other life is hinted, and no ask, decide or recap changes beyond those lines, with pypdf and without. It is in `ask` and not the week because a week step takes no question of its own.
