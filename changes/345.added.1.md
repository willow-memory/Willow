`secure-sum` totals several people's kart results by Shamir's secret sharing (shamirs): `split` cuts a sealed result into one share per person in the round, sent straight to each; `combine` adds the shares a person holds; `total` puts the sums together, any T of them, checking the rest. The one who asks learns the total, and no package holds anyone's own count; a forwarded share, a sum from someone not at its place, and sums that disagree are refused
