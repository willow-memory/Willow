The `ran` row records the SHA-256 of bash and each program the job names, a result's claim carries their hash, and `gather` says when results ran on different tools. demo/johnson's first job is `grep -rli`, not `-rlic`, which busybox's grep read as a count and listed a file with no Johnson in it
